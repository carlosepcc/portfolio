## Featured Project

### Carlito — Native Android Utility Hub for Daily Life in Cuba

Sole developer · Kotlin, Jetpack Compose · In beta (0.x) · Published on Apklis and Zapstore

An Android app I designed, architected and developed solo. It started as an experiment to organise PAGOxMOVIL banking SMS and grew into a full utility hub:

- **Pizarra:** ETECSA balance dashboard — main balance, data, voice, bonuses and SMS with validity dates — queried over USSD, with dual-SIM support.
- **Banca:** Bank accounts with biometric-protected PIN storage, transfers and online payments.
- **Mensajes:** Inbox organised by sender (ETECSA, ENZONA, Nauta, CUBACEL) instead of a chronological dump.
- Architecture documented as living documents alongside the code, covering USSD encoding, dual-SIM handling, online payments and UI modularisation. A Flutter migration is in progress.
